## 📌 TL;DR

1. **批十六预告的四项，本轮最重的发现不在四项里——BC 官方 A1-A2 索引 18 课逐条点目后，我方**尚余 4 处「课程位空缺」**（`-ed/-ing` 形容词／`few·a few·little·a bit of`／`have got`／物主 `'s`），且这 4 处在 **Murphy 双册各有独立单元**（中级 U98／初级 U84·中级 U87／初级 U9·中级 U17／初级 U64·中级 U81）＝**A 档（跨源官方课位）**，不是「词典型·中文需求驱动」。
2. **`have sth done` 维持撤出**：Murphy **中级 U46**（跨级）；BC **A1-A2 18 课／B1-B2 36 课／C1 14 课三档全目零课位**（`Passives` 页 B1+B2 明查无此格、`Advanced passives review` 无）；我方招牌句四个零件（`have/had`＋`hair`＋`cut`）**全库零**、`will have` **0** → **C 档，不入章**。
3. **`be/get used to` 的跨源位置比批十六记录更硬**：BC **B1-B2 有课程位**且是**一页同教三张脸**（`Different uses of 'used to'`）；Murphy **中级 U61 独立单元**（标题即 `be/get used to`）；Cambridge **两处落点**（`used-to` 页含 "Used to or be used to?" 段 ＋ **Common mistakes 专页**两条明文 ❌）；中文侧**两篇专文＋明文负迁移**（❌ `She used to working late.`）。**但属 B1 段，且与我方 L93 已教明的「to 后面永远穿原样」正面冲突** → **A- 档但列备选**。
4. **`look like` 三度三缺维持**（BC **B1-B2**、中文侧**四度零专文**、我方 `looks like` 仅 L49 一句天气义对话）；**但本轮新增一条区别**：`look + 形容词` 我方实有 **12 处认读**（L13/19/48/51/66/71/72/76/79/83/87/92，**全在 NPC 对白、从不作 target**）——「look 家族」不是纯零，是**只认读不教**，两者应分开评估。
5. **推荐乙「A2 收尾缺口补课章」8 课（L111–L118，单拱）**；甲（B1 开局）列备选并建议写为**批十八首选项**；丙不入章；**双拱不需要声明**（首选与备选一均单拱）。

**课程／参考页要点（本轮实读）**
- **`Different uses of 'used to'`（B1 Intermediate＋B2 Upper intermediate）＝本批最关键单页**：一页教**两套**——① `used to + 不定式`（"a past situation that is no longer true"，例 "She used to be a long-distance runner when she was younger."／"I didn't use to sleep very well…"／"Did you use to come here as a child?"）；② **`be used to` / `get used to` 都在**——"Be used to means 'be familiar with' or 'be accustomed to'." 例 "She's used to the city now…"／"He wasn't used to walking so much…"／"I'm a teacher so I'm used to speaking in public."；"get used to" 表过程，例 "I'm finding this new job hard but I'm sure I'll get used to it soon."；明写**后接 "a noun, pronoun or the -ing form of a verb"**；并提示 "The question form of used to is regular so it is **didn't use to**."
- **`Past habits: 'used to', 'would' and the past simple`（B1＋B2）**：明白警告 "**should not be confused with be/get used to + -ing, which has a different meaning**"；`would` 只用于重复动作（"Every Saturday I would go on a long bike ride."）、**不用于状态**；past simple 永远是替代方案，"If something happened only once, we must use the past simple."
- **`Passives`（B1＋B2）**：只讲 `be + past participle`（含各时态与情态），**通篇无 have something done**。
- **`active-passive-voice`（参考层三档）**：beginner／intermediate／advanced 三段；**只有一条评论里出现 "causative 'have' construction…sometimes described as a pseudo-passive construction"**——**构造本身不在正文**（批十六结论维持，本轮再证）。
- **`Present simple: 'have got'`（A1 Elementary＋A2 Pre-intermediate）＝have got 的课位依据**：明写 "We use I/you/we/they + **have got** or he/she/it + **has got** to talk about things that we have."；"**have** and **have got** mean the same, with **have got** being a little less formal"（口语多、书面少）；用途含 appearance／family／possessions／timetabled events／illnesses／abstract；例句 "I've got a big family."／"We haven't got a garden."／"Have you got any pets?"／"She has got a good job."；否定与疑问的完整形式表；**关键限制**："Have/has got is only used in the present tense. For the past, use **had** without got."
- **`Adjectives ending in '-ed' and '-ing'`（A1＋A2）**：-ed "describe emotions, telling how people feel"（"I was so bored in that lesson, I almost fell asleep."／"She was really tired and went to bed early."）；-ing "describe the thing causing the emotion"（"It's really frightening."／"She's so interesting."／"That noise is really annoying!"）；**成对清单 10 组**：annoyed/annoying、**bored/boring**、confused/confusing、disappointed/disappointing、**excited/exciting**、frightened/frightening、**interested/interesting**、surprised/surprising、tired/tiring、worried/worrying。**无 "Not:" 错例**。
- **`Quantifiers: 'few', 'a few', 'little' and 'a bit of'`（A1＋A2）**：四个对位——`a few`（＋复数，＝some）／`a bit of`·`a little`（＋不可数）＝「够或比预期多」；`few`（＋复数）／`very little`（＋不可数）＝「不够或比预期少」；并注明 "little without 'very' is less common and sounds quite formal"；例句 "I have a few friends, so I'm not lonely." 并排 "She has few friends, so she's quite lonely."／"We've got a bit of time before our train." 并排 "We've got very little time before our train. Hurry up!"。**无 "Not:" 错例**。
- **`Possessive 's`（A1＋A2）**：`'s` 表关系与归属，**单数 ＋'s／复数词尾 s ＋'／不规则复数 ＋'s／词尾 s 的名字 ' 或 's 皆可**；可**连串**（"my friend's father's house"）；名单只在最后一名加（"Liam is Anne and Gary's son."）；例句 "Mohammed is my brother's son."／"My grandpa's beard is white."／"This is my grandparents' house."／"Our friends' house is in the mountains."／"The women's clothes are on the second floor."。**该页不覆盖 mine/yours／a friend of mine**。
- **`Adjectives and prepositions`（A1＋A2）＝本批新发现的第五处缺口**：六族完整清单——**at**（技能）good/bad/amazing/brilliant/terrible at；**about**（感受）angry/excited/happy/nervous/sad/stressed/worried about；**of** afraid/frightened/scared of、**proud of**；**to** married/addicted/allergic/similar to、friendly/nice/polite/rude to；**for** good/bad/**famous for**/responsible for；**in** **interested in**/experienced/involved in。明写「无规则，跟着句子一起记」。**无 "Not:" 错例**。
- 另读：`clause-structure-verb-patterns`（**elementary**，无使役、无 look like）；`delexical-verbs…`（**beginner**，"have a bath / take a break / make a noise"，causative have 只在读者评论里）；`talking-about-past`（**intermediate**，`used to`／`would`／past simple 三分，**不含 be used to**，含 "NOT would look" 类错型）；`present-tense`／`talking-about-present`（intermediate／advanced；**均无 have got 对比、无系动词 look+形容词**）；`verb-phrases`（beginner＋intermediate＋advanced；**无 look like／look+形容词**）；`where-adjectives-go-in-a-sentence`（**beginner**）："That film **looks interesting**."（**look＋形容词的参考层落点**，但 only be/look/feel 三词，sound/smell/taste 未列）；`using-as-like`（B1+B2，本机缓存实读，"**He looks like his dad.**"／"**She looks like her mother.**"＋感官动词排比 "It sounds like a cat."／"It smells like medicine."／"It feels like cotton."）。

### B. Cambridge Dictionary（有原文·6 页）

- **`have-something-done`（Grammar > Verbs > Passive voice；无 CEFR）＝四格一页**：① 请人做（`have + object + -ed`，"We're having the house painted next week."，强调过程不强调谁做）＋**明文警告** "This pattern is **not the same as the present perfect or past perfect**" 并四句 Compare（"I had my hair cut." vs "I've cut my hair."／"I'd cut my hair."／"I cut my own hair."）；② 倒霉事（"They've had their car stolen."／"Hundreds of people had their homes destroyed by the hurricane."）；③ 差遣（`have + object + 原形`，"I'll have Harry book you a taxi."／"He had Kay make us all some tea."）；④ 经历（`-ing` 表进行 "We had a man singing to us…"、原形表完成 "We had a strange woman come to the door selling pictures."＋"Her story had us laughing so much."）。**无 "Not:" 错例**。
- **`used-to`（无 CEFR）＝used to 与 be used to 的对照页**：used to 表「不再是事实的过去」，含否定（`didn't use(d) to` 最常见、"used not to" 正式）、疑问、**emphatic did**（"we did used to say hello"）、tags（"did he?"）。**明文 ❌ 三条**："We are used to go…"／"We use to go…"／"We were used to go…"（皆在 "In statements, the form used to does not change. We do not use the verb be before it." 之下）；**`would` 段落明文** "Not: We would live in Manchester."／"Not: 'The Townhouse' would be a Greek restaurant…"；**专设 "Used to or be used to?" 段**："Be used to means 'be accustomed to' or 'be familiar with'. It can refer to the past, present or future. We follow **be used to** with a noun phrase, a pronoun or the **-ing** form of a verb"，例 "I work in a hospital, so I'm used to long hours."／"She's not used to it."／"He was a salesman, so he was used to travelling up and down the country."／"You'll soon get used to it."（并提 become used to）。
- **`word-choice-used-to-and-be-used-to`（Common mistakes in English > Word choice；本轮新取）＝明文负迁移专页**：`Used to + verb` 表过去常态／习惯；**"When you make used to + verb into a question or negative using the verb do, the correct form is use to."**（正确 "My dad didn't use to smoke."／"Where did you use to live?"；**错例 "Where did you **used** to live?"**）；`be used to something/doing something` 表熟悉；**错例 "He's not **use** to working long hours." → 正 "He's not used to working long hours."**。
- **`look`（无 CEFR）**：系动词用法 "That picture looks old."／"That jacket looks very expensive."（说明其后接 complement、**不带宾语**）；**`look like + 名词短语`**："He looks like someone famous."／"It looks like a nice day outside."；**`look as if/as though + 从句**"："She looks as if she is going to cry."／"They looked as though they had seen a ghost."；**有 "Not:" 错例但都不在系动词段**（"He's looking at me." vs "Not: He's looking me."；"look if/whether" 禁用，用 see if）。`look forward to` 在此页只是导航链接。
- **`have-got`（无 CEFR）＝have got 的词典级依据**："Have got and have mean the same. **Have got is more informal.**"；用途＝possession／relationships／characteristics／illnesses（**这些义项不用进行式**）；成对例句 "I've got a terrible pain in my back."／"They haven't got a car."／"She's got two cats and a dog."／"Have you got a drill?"／"How many brothers have you got?"；**疑问／否定双轨对照**：have got 直接倒装／直接加 not，have 走 do（have 版标 "(more formal)"）；**明文 "Not:"** "Not: She **is having got** two cats and a dog."／"Not: She is having a headache."
- **`be-used-to-get-used-to` 无独立页**：该 URL 落在 Grammar hub（仅分类链接，正文无此条）——**「be/get used to 专页」在 Cambridge 不存在**，内容并入 `used-to` 与 `word-choice-…` 两页（**勿误记**）。

### D. 中文侧 english.cool（有原文·869 篇目录 ＋ 本轮直读 12 篇）

- **`used-to`（专文）＝「三张脸」中文第一文**：used to＝「過去經常做的行為或重覆發生的狀態，但現在已經不再發生」＋`used to + 原形動詞`（明写「**注意：to 為不定詞，因此後面只能接原形動詞**」）；否定两式（`didn't use to`／正式 `used not to`）＋`never used to`；**`be used to`**："代表「（某人）習慣某事物」"＋**明写「特別留意，be used to 的 to 是介系詞，後面的字須為名詞的形式唷，與 used to 並不同」**＋`be used to + N./V-ing`＋副词修饰（"I'm very used to…"／"pretty used to…"）；**`get used to`**："可等同 become used to…由於 get 本身有「漸漸」的意思…是「開始習慣某事」"；**5 题小試身手**（含 getting used to／weren't used to／were used to 三脸混排）。**内容瑕疵（记录，不作依据）**：第 ④ 题解析写「故答案為 **didn't used to**」，与前文「正確形式是 didn't use to」自相矛盾。
- **`habit-be-used-to`（专文·本轮新取）＝be/get used to 的中文最强单篇**：名词侧 habit/custom/routine 三分解；动词侧 `be used to`（已習慣的狀態）／`get used to`（**逐漸習慣的過程**）；**明文负迁移**：**「She is used to working late. ⭕️ ／ She used to working late. ❌」**（并解释「想表達『習慣某事』一定要有 be 動詞、後面接 V-ing。沒有 be 動詞的 used to 後面只能接原形動詞，而且意思會變成『以前都⋯』」）；另附 `accustomed to`（正式版）；末尾总表把 habit／be used to／get used to／used to／custom／routine／accustomed to 七项排一行。
- **`causative-verbs`（专文）＝have sth done 的中文唯一系统落点**：let/make/have 三主动（明写「它們的受詞後面都是加**原形動詞**」）＋get 单列（"後面不是加原形動詞，而是加 **to V**"）；**明文负迁移**："這如果沒有特別去記，**學生會容易把後面的動詞一律寫成 to V 或 V-ing 的錯誤用法**"；**同场景四连排**（"I'll let him go out with friends."／"I'll make him go to meet the girl."／"I'll have him go to the meeting."／"I'll get him to go to the gym."）；**被动段三例**——"I had my hair cut.（我剪頭髮了）"／"I'll have the laptop checked asap."／"He had his smartphone fixed."＋get 版 "We got our house painted last year."／"The team got the report revised."；**受害型**："I had/got my cell phone stolen."／"She had/got her leg broken."，并自注「學習者多半很陌生」。
- **`emotive-verbs`（＝bored VS boring 专文）＝-ed/-ing 形容词的中文最强单篇**：情绪动词定义（excite/bore/interest）＋句构「物 + 情緒動詞 + 人」；**-ing「令人感到…」修饰人与物／P.P.「感到…」只修饰人**；**明文 ❌/⭕ 对**："I find it **interesting** to travel around the island by scooter. ⭕ ／ I am **interesting** to travel around the island by scooter. ❌"；**15 组成对清单**（boring/bored、touching/touched、surprising/surprised、confusing/confused、frustrating/frustrated、tiring/tired、worrying/worried、depressing/depressed、annoying/annoyed、interesting/interested、embarrassing/embarrassed、exciting/excited、shocking/shocked、relaxing/relaxed、exhausting/exhausted）；**末段搭配表 14 条**（bored **with**／touched **by**／surprised **at**·**by**／confused **about**·**by**／tired **of**／worried **about**／interested **in**／scared **of**／excited **about**／shocked **at**·**by**…）＝**形容词＋介词的中文素材**。
- **`quantifiers`（专文）**：数量词总表（some／any／**a few**／**a little**／**few**／**little**）＋例句（"I saw a few people there."／"I have a little money."／"**Few** people knew the news."／"There is **little** juice…"）；`both` 段（both A and B）；**无明文 ❌ 负迁移**。
- **`possessive`（专文）＝物主 's 的中文单篇**：四类所有格（人称代词所有格／名词所有格 `'s`／事物的 of／双重所有格）；**名词所有格四条变化表**（单数 ＋'s／复数词尾 s ＋'／复数非 s ＋'s／词尾 s 名 ' 或 's）；**明文负迁移两条**：① **"Here are your sister's some books. ❌ 這樣的寫法是中式英文唷"**（所有格与限定词不得并存 → "some books of your sister's ⭕"）；② **"That bicycle of Mike was stolen. ❌ ／ That bicycle of Mike's was stolen. ⭕"**；并注 of 后须生物（"a friend of the school's ❌"）。
- **`a-an-the`（专文）**：a/an 与 the 的分工；**明文负迁移三条**："I bought book. ❌"（可数名词不能光着）／"I bought a milk. ❌"（不可数不用 a/an）／"I bought milks. ❌"；"the" 用「聽者知道是哪一本」解释。
- **`seem`（专文·作为 look 家族邻居）**：seem＋形容词／seem to＋动词／**seem to have + p.p.**／There seems to be＋名词／It seems like＋子句／It seems as if·as though／It seems that；**明文对比**：appear 不能接 like/as if/as though（"❌ It appears like you're depressed. ⭕ It seems like you're depressed."）。
- **`like`（专文）**：like 当「喜欢」（to V 与 V-ing 两可、持续情境多用 V-ing）；**全站 look like 仅 2 例句、无教学段**（"Your son doesn't look **like** you."／"You look **like** you just saw a ghost."）——**look like 专文四度确认零**。
- 另读：`interest-interested-interesting-interest-in`（interested in ＋ ❌ "I am interesting to travel…"）、`participles`（p.p. 定义，术语密度高）、`passive`（缓存 body：全时态被动表，**含 have been pp；无 have sth done**）、`where-…` 等。
