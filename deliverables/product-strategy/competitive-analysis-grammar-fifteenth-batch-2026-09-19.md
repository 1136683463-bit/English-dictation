## ① 复核结果

**BC（有原文·三档全目）**：**A1-A2＝18 课**，含 **`Past continuous and past simple`**（正文标 **A1 Elementary／A2 Pre-intermediate**："The past continuous and the past simple help us to show how two past actions or situations are connected."；"the past simple action happened in the middle of the past continuous action, while it was in progress."；**成对例 "When the guests arrived, Jane was cooking dinner."／"…Jane cooked dinner."**；另含 "When I woke up this morning, it was snowing."／"I was sleeping when you called me."／"While I was studying, I suddenly felt sleepy."／"As I was going to work, I saw an old friend."；双测）；**无使役课**。**B1-B2＝36 课**：过去进行**零**；used to 两课均标 B1+B2（`Past habits…` 含 "Every Saturday I would go on a long bike ride."＋"is often used in stories"）；**无使役课**。**C1＝14 课**：**无 have/get sth done、无 make/let sb do**。**参考层**：`'to'-infinitives`（beginner/intermediate/advanced）**无 make/let/help＋原形**；`delexical verbs`＝beginner 仅名词搭配（"They made arrangements…"）；`clause-structure-verb-patterns` 无使役；`verbs-time-clauses-if-clauses`＝beginner＋intermediate；`past tense`＝intermediate（"Past continuous: I was working"＋as 例）。

**Cambridge Dictionary（有原文·10 页）**：**使役六页**——`infinitives-without-to`（"the infinitive without to after **let, make and (optionally) help**"）；**`make`**（"In the active voice…without to: The boss made me work an extra day. **Not: The boss made me to work**…in the passive voice, we must use an infinitive with to: The people were made to wait…"＋**make＋宾语＋形容词** "Music makes me happy."）；**`let`**（"**Let is followed by an object and an infinitive without to**"）；**`help somebody (to) do`**（"with or without to"＋"**We don't use help with an -ing form**"）；**`have something done`**（"have + object + -ed form"／"have + object + infinitive without to: I'll have Harry book you a taxi."）；**`hear, see, etc.`**（原形/-ing 分工＋❌"She heard the doorbell to ring"）。**均无 CEFR**。**叙事四页**——`past-continuous-or-past-simple`（"[background event] I was listening to the radio **when** [main event] Helen phoned."／"the past continuous for the background event"）；`when`（"When I was young…"）；`while`（同时义＋**❌during I study**）；`used-to`（否定 `didn't use(d) to`＋疑问＋**used to or would／or be used to**）。

**Murphy（有原文·本机 TOC 原件）**：**初级（115 单元）**——**U13「I was doing」／U14「I was doing and I did」＝书首 12%**；U35 仅 **Let's**；U36「I used to…」；U55/U57 delexical 与 do/make 辨析；U97–102 连词块（U98「When…」）；U105「before after during while」＝**介词**单元。**无 make/let＋原形单元**（make 2 处均非使役、help 零）。**中级（145 单元）**——**U6「Past continuous」＝前 4%**；U18「used to (do)」；**U25「when I do and when I've done」＋if and when（现在/将来）**；U42–45 被动块→**U46「have something done」＝两册唯一使役族单元**；U56/U57（try/need/**help**）；U61 be/get used to；**U67「see somebody do/doing」＝感官动词**；**U116 as（as I walked/as I was）／U119 during for while／U120 by and until**；**全书 make 零命中**。

**中文侧 english.cool（有原文）**：**使役三篇**——`causative-verbs`（let/make/have/get 排比＋have/get＋p.p. 被动段＋**负迁移自认**「學生會容易把後面的動詞一律寫成 to V 或 V-ing」＋**同场景四连排** "I'll let him go out／make him go／have him go／get him to go"）；`make`（"make + sb + 原形動詞…寫成 **make sb to do，這是錯的 ❌**"＋"My boss made me work overtime again."）；`help`（"help + 人 + **(to) + 原形**"）；邻页 `sense-verbs`（see/hear 后 **原V vs V-ing**＋❌"I saw her to dance"）。**叙事四篇**——`past-continuous-tense`（长动作 vs 短动作＋**三句同义改写组** "I was watching TV when Mom called.／Mom called when I was watching TV.／…while…"＋**四组同句两版对比**）；`when-while`（可混用/不可混用两分法＋**5 题填空测验**）；`when`（"When I called Yvonne, she was watching a movie."）；`while-meanwhile`（句首逗号＋**主句主语相同可省略**）。`used-to`（三兄弟＋否定三式＋小試身手 5 题）。
