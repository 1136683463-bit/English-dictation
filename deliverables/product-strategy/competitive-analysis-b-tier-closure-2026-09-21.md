### 1.1 `several` —— **维持不做（B−）**

**② 跨源逐字**：
- Cambridge `/grammar/british-grammar/determiners-and-types-of-noun`（**title 已核** `Determiners and types of noun - Cambridge Grammar`）："**Both, many, (a) few, the numbers two, three, four, etc., several, these and those are only used with plural nouns:**" ＋ 例 `Several items were missing when we opened the box to unpack everything.`
- Cambridge `/grammar/british-grammar/determiners-used-as-pronouns`（**title 已核**）："**Determiners which can be used as pronouns in this way are:**" ＋ 名单含 **`several`** ＋ 例 `It's not just one school that is in financial difficulty; there are several.`
- Cambridge 词典 `/dictionary/english/several`（**`epp-xref dxref A2` span 实存 ⇒ 标位可用**）：词性 `determiner, pronoun`，**A2**，定义 "**some; an amount that is not exact but is fewer than many:**"
- **中文侧 `english.cool/several/` → 404**（sitemap 873 条全索引，无专文）

**③ 维持理由**：**① 两句都是「同句并列」型**（塞进名单，非独立规则句）；**② 零禁用**（两页 `Not:` 均不含 `several`）；**③ 3 标记约束不可满足**（`several`/`a few`/`some` 三选一语法全对，标不出错）。**⇒ 维持批三十四的「B− ＋ 处置改 L114 的 3 张刻度卡」。**
**④ 与并发 21 课**：**无撞车无空间**（最近邻居 L166 `too many`／L162 `most of` 均非同轴）。

### 1.3 `as if` —— **维持（B，缓排）**

**② 跨源逐字**：
- Cambridge `/grammar/british-grammar/as-if-and-as-though`（**⚠️ `/as-if` 与 `/as-though` 两 slug 都 200，指向同一页**）：**H2 实为 0（整页连续正文）**；**`Not: count = 0`**、Warning 1。逐字："**As if and as though are conjunctions.**"／"**We use as if and as though to make comparisons. **③ 维持理由**：**BC 落 C1**（我方是零基础 A1→A2 线）＋ **零禁用** ＋ **与 L159 抢 `look` 槽位**（**L159 逐字 `grammarLabel: "看起来像 · look 后面跟 like"`；L160 逐字 `"好像 · seem 后面跟 to"`**，而 Cambridge 逐字 "commonly follow the verbs **feel** and **look**"）。

### 2.6 `had to`（不得不，昨天版）—— **⚠️ 不是同义换词 ⇒ B＋（本批最高）**

#### ⚠️ 2.6.0 回答「`had to` 是否只是 `have to`（L16）与「昨天版」概念的合成」

**本轮结果：⚠️ 不是。上游用 `Warning:` 框逐字规定「过去义务不用 `must`，用 `had to`」——这是硬规则，而我方从未教过。**

**依据 1 — Cambridge `Must` 页两次逐字规定（本轮实取）**。`/grammar/british-grammar/must` → **200**，title 逐字 **`Must - Grammar - Cambridge Dictionary`**。

> **（`Must: typical errors` 节）** "**We don't use must to express obligation and necessity in the past. We use had to instead:**" ＋ `When she got home, she had to cook dinner before everyone arrived.` ＋ **`Not: When she got home, she must cook dinner before …`**

**⇒ 三要点**：① **2 条正面规则 ＋ 3 条 `Not:`（8 项里禁用最多）**；② 它是「禁止型」硬规则（`must` 过去义务不存在 ⇒ `had to` 是唯一形式）；③ **顺带发现 `by the time` 出现在该页例句里**（`By the time we got back to our bikes …`）——**两条轴在同一句里的天然合并点。**

- **① 课程位 ❌**（BC 68 课 0）／**② 规则页 ✅ 极厚（本批最厚）**（`So` 页两节 ＋ **`Such or so?` 页 `Not:` 10** ＋ `Such` 页 ＋ LDOCE 语法框 ＋ 中文侧两篇）

### 2.8 `such a`（这样一个）—— **B−（与 `so ... that` 同轴，不单独开课）**

### 4.3 课 3：`so ... that` ＋ `such a` —— **B，顺位 3（1 课 2 格）**

> **跨源支撑 5 件**（逐字见 §2.7）：① `So` 页 `So + adjective` 节 **"We often use so with that:"** ＋ `It was so dark (that) we could hardly see.`（**⚠️ 括号说明 `that` 可省，本课一条真规则**）；② `So` 页 `So and that-clauses` 节 **"We do not use very in this structure:"** ＋ **`Not: They drove very fast that …`**；③ `Such` 页 **"We use such before the indefinite article, a/an:"** ＋ **`Not: We had a such awful meal …`**；④ **`Such or so?` 页（`Not:` 10 条，本批最多）** 的 `Compare` 表与四条 `Typical errors`；⑤ 中文侧 `letmeenglish` 三个 h3 并列表 ＋ `english.cool` 收尾速查（**`so that（目的）→ 中間不夾字`** vs **`so… that…（結果）→ so 接形容詞`**）→ **与 L186 切分的现成口诀**。
