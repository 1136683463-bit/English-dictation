## 📌 TL;DR

1. **甲是唯一「跨源课程级空白」**：BC 三档全目逐条点目 **68 课零使役**；Murphy **make 零命中**（使役族唯一单元＝中级 **U46 have something done**）；Cambridge 使役族 **10 页**；我方 `make/makes/making` GL **0**、`lets` **0**、`get sb to do` **0**。短板＝课程位零＋`let` 宾语 50/51 全 `me`（L74 占 49）＝**语义位半重叠**。
2. **乙的空白在 until／as，不在 during**：Cambridge **`until` 页（六类典型错误）＝本轮最强单页**；BC **B1-B2 有课程位** `Using 'as' and 'like'`（as='while'）；Murphy **U119 during for while／U120 by and until by the time**。但 **during 已由 L98 讲透**（4 讲解＋1 ❌ 卡＋summary 明文）、`while` 已教 83 处 → **单开即重复；乙总量 2–3 课，不足 6 课**。
3. **丙跨级**：U46＝中级；BC `Passives`(B1+B2)／`Advanced passives review`(C1) **均无**，仅参考层承认 "causative have"；我方 `will have` 0、`have+物主+名词+PP` 0 → **不入章**。
4. **丁容量 8–10 但双线**，与批十三单锚、批十五单拱形态相悖 → **次选**。
5. **推荐甲「使役补完章」单拱 8 课（L103–L110）**，乙的 until＋as 折入章尾 1 课。

## ① 复核结果

**BC（有原文·三档全目＋参考层九页）**
- **A1-A2＝18 课**：仅 `Past continuous and past simple`（**A1＋A2**："When the guests arrived, Jane **was cooking** dinner."／"…Jane **cooked** dinner."＋"**As** I was going to work, I saw an old friend."）与 `Verbs followed by -ing or infinitive` 相关；**无使役、无 during／until／as 课**。
- **B1-B2＝36 课**：唯一相关 `Using 'as' and 'like'`（**B1＋B2**）——as 表 **'while'／'during the time that'**："She called **as I was getting out of the bath**."；**无使役课**；`Passives`（B1＋B2）**无 have sth done**。
- **C1＝14 课**：`Advanced passives review` 仅 get 被动＋impersonal passive，**无 have sth done、无使役**。
- **参考层**：`infinitives`（三档**均无 make/let＋原形**）；`clause-structure-verb-patterns`（**elementary**，无使役）；`delexical verbs`（make 仅 "They made arrangements…"）；`verbs-time-clauses-if-clauses`（**beginner＋intermediate**："You must wait here **until** your father comes."）；`adverbials-time`（**beginner**："There was a storm **during** the night."／"from 1 July **until** 3 August"）；`active-passive-voice`（承认 "**causative have** construction"）；`past-tense`（intermediate）。

**Cambridge Dictionary（有原文·11 页）**
- **使役族**：`infinitives-without-to`；**`make`** 一义一格（＋object／＋adj "Music makes me happy."／＋noun／＋io＋do／＋pp）＋**"force to do" 独立段**："The boss made me work an extra day. **Not: The boss made me to work**…passive: The people were made to wait…"；`let`（"**Let is followed by an object and an infinitive without to**"／❌"She let me to look"／被动禁用）；`help somebody (to) do`（"with or without to"／"**We don't use help with an -ing form**"）；`have something done`（-ed 格 "We're having the house painted next week."／**"not the same as the present perfect"** 四句 Compare／＋原形 "I'll have Harry book you a taxi."）；`hear, see, etc.`（❌"She heard the doorbell to ring"）；**`get`**（"**a less formal way of saying have something done**"／"They got me to talk to the police."）；`have`（**无 have sth done**）；`verb-patterns`（索引）。**均无 CEFR**。
- **时间侧**：**`until` 页＝最强**＋**六类典型错误**：by（"finished **by** 9 pm. Not: …until 9 pm."）／up to／as far as／before／**语序**（"Not: Until the talk ended no one left…"）／**时态**（"until the summer holidays **begin**. Not: …will begin."／"till Donna **has finished**."）＋from…until＋拼写 one l。**`during`**（"before nouns and noun phrases"＋与 **for** 分工＋"**Not: During I was waiting for the bus**"）。**`as`**（连词四义：**当…时**"arrived **as we were leaving**"＋"often in the continuous form"／因为／如同／同时变化 "**As you get older**…**Not: While you get older…**"）。**`by`**＋**by the time**（"By the time you wake up, I'll have finished work!"）。**`Conjunctions: time`**（when/after/before/until/since/while/once/as/as soon as 总表＋❌will＋"Not: …during I study"）。

**中文侧 english.cool（有原文·12 篇）**
- **使役**：`causative-verbs`——let/make/have 三主动＋get 单列（"後面不是加原形動詞，而是加 **to V**"）＋**明文负迁移**（"學生會容易把後面的動詞一律寫成 to V 或 V-ing 的錯誤用法"）＋**同场景四连排**（"let him go out／make him go／have him go／**get him to go**"）＋被动段（"I had my hair cut."）＋受害型（"I had/got my cell phone stolen."）；`make`——"寫成 **make sb to do，這是錯的 ❌**"＋"My boss **made me work** overtime again."；`help`（明文争议"help 真的是使役動詞嗎？"）；`sense-verbs`（❌"I saw her to dance"）；`passive`（**无 have sth done**）。
- **时间侧**：**`until`**——"持續的動作" vs "not until…**瞬間或短暫性的動作**"＋三式互转（"Not until X **did she** meet…"）＋**同句两版对比**（"was working until 9:09" vs "did not start working until 9:09"）＋**小試身手 5 题**；`until-vs-till`（"完全可以互換…差在語氣的正式程度"）；`during`（"是介系詞，後面都要接名詞"）；`as`（六大用法，**3. 當…時**）；`while-meanwhile`（逗号规则＋可省略）；`conjunctions`（时间组含 **until**）；`by`（"**By the time** he came home, his wife had gone to bed."）。

## ⑤ 本轮口径校正（勿沿用旧记）

1. **「乙＝during／as／until 三连词」→ 校正**：**during 已由 L98 讲透**（4 讲解＋1 ❌ 卡＋summary），单开即重复；乙真空白＝**until（全 0）＋as 连词（`As＋主语` 0）**，共约 2–3 课。
2. **「as 无课程位」→ 细化**：BC **B1-B2 有课程位** `Using 'as' and 'like'`（**B1＋B2**），as=**'while'**；A1-A2 层仅课内例证 → **as 跨源位置高于 during／until**。
3. **Murphy 使役口径补全**：make 零命中＋唯一使役族单元 **U46**（维持）；**U46 归丙**，甲的邻位＝U35／U54／U55／U56／U57。
4. **Cambridge 使役族 10 页**：新增 **`get` 页**（"a less formal way of saying have something done"）；**`have` 页无 have sth done**（勿误记）。
5. **until 页为乙侧最强单页（本轮新发现）**：六类典型错误＋from…until＋拼写，**强度高于 during 页**。
6. **`by the time` 是 until 的天然对照**：Cambridge `by` 页＋中文 `by` 第 2 类＋Murphy **U120** 三方同页；我方 `by the time` 0、`by` 时间义 0。
